========== Recipe Details ==========
Recipe Name: Lemon Juice
Preparation Time: 5 minutes
Cooking Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 1 glass

Ingredients:
- 1 medium lemon
- 250 ml cold water
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- A pinch of salt (optional)
- Ice cubes (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Wash the lemon thoroughly with water.
2. Cut the lemon into two halves.
3. Squeeze the lemon and remove the seeds.
4. Pour 250 ml of cold water into a glass.
5. Add 2 teaspoons of sugar and stir well.
6. Add a pinch of salt if desired.
7. Add ice cubes and serve.

========== Recipe Details ==========
Recipe Name: Tomato Rice
Preparation Time: 10 minutes
Cooking Time: 20-25 minutes
Servings: 2 people

Ingredients:
- 1 cup raw rice
- 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1 green chilli
- 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala (optional)
- A few curry leaves
- Salt to taste
- 1 3/4 to 2 cups water, as needed
- Coriander leaves (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Wash the rice two or three times and drain the water.
2. Chop the tomatoes and slice the onion.
3. Heat oil in a pot or pressure cooker.
4. Add mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.
5. Add curry leaves, onion and green chilli.
6. Saute until the onion becomes soft.
7. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.
8. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
9. Add turmeric powder, chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
10. Add the washed rice and mix gently.
11. Pour in the appropriate amount of water.
12. Cover and cook until the rice is tender.
13. Release pressure safely if using a pressure cooker.
14. Let the rice rest for 5 minutes.
15. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve.

========== Recipe Details ==========
Recipe Name: Chocolate Milkshake
Preparation Time: 5 minutes
Cooking Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 1 large glass

Ingredients:
- 250 ml chilled milk
- 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
- 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
- 1-2 teaspoons sugar (optional)
- 1 scoop chocolate or vanilla ice cream (optional)
- Chocolate shavings (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Make sure the blender jar is clean.
2. Pour chilled milk into the blender.
3. Add chocolate syrup and cocoa powder.
4. Add sugar if desired.
5. Add ice cream for a creamier texture, if desired.
6. Close the blender lid securely.
7. Blend for 30-45 seconds until smooth.
8. Drizzle chocolate syrup inside a glass.
9. Pour the milkshake into the glass.
10. Garnish with chocolate shavings and serve immediately.
